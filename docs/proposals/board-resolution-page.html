    9 · Decided

    Decisions

    Farhath accepted these proposals on 30 Oct 2026. The SBC team can still change them.

    #QuestionDecision
    D1Does a foreign parent's resolution need a notary and an apostille?Yes. They are two steps. SBC can turn them off for a client.
    D2Is the NOC separate from the board resolution?No. The NOC is the parent's board resolution: one document in three versions (foreign, Indian name use, Indian investing). Title: "Board resolution (NOC)". This follows VCFO Suite's code.
    D3Who certifies the true copy?A director selected from the parent's director list.
    D4Does an Indian parent follow the same steps?Yes, without the notary and apostille steps.
    D5Does the client see how VCFO chose the template?Yes. The full tree, with the chosen path and the answers behind it.
