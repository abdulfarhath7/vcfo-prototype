1 · Summary

What I understood

WhereVCFO Suite › Post incorporation › Board meetings. Each client company has its own list of meetings.
WhatEach meeting needs an agenda: the list of items that the meeting discusses and decides.
TemplateThe agenda has two parts. Common items occur in every meeting, for example quorum and the last minutes. Purpose items change with the type of meeting, for example the quarterly results or the annual accounts.
MeetingsTypes include the first board meeting, quarterly board meetings, the annual accounts meeting, the AGM, the EGM and special meetings. Section 3 gives the full list.
The screenThe SBC team showed a Zoho pop-up with two boxes. The right box holds all the agenda items. The left box holds this meeting's agenda. An arrow between them moves items from right to left. Drag and drop does the same.
When it opensThe user opens an existing meeting or creates a new meeting. The pop-up opens with all the items on the right.
Next stepThe SBC team checks this page and sends changes. Then we build the feature in the prototype and in VCFO Suite.
I prepared the legal references from the Companies Act 2013 and the Secretarial Standards SS-1 (board meetings) and SS-2 (general meetings). SBC's company secretary must check them before we build.

6 · Proposals

Ideas for the SBC team

These ideas add to the two-box picker. Tell us which ones to keep.

IdeaWhat it doesWhy it helps
Year planVCFO creates the four board meetings of the financial year, plus the AGM.No meeting is forgotten.
120-day guardVCFO shows the last date for the next board meeting. It warns when the gap is near 120 days.Section 173(1) sets the limit.
Smart suggestionsThe first meeting of the financial year suggests MBP-1 and DIR-8. The meeting before the AGM suggests the accounts.The correct items come to the top.
Decision → filingEach item knows its form and its due date. After the meeting, VCFO adds the filing to Compliances › Obligations.One decision creates one tracked task.
DocumentsVCFO makes the notice, the agenda and the draft minutes from the chosen items.The team types less.
Draft resolutionsEach item holds standard resolution text. The team edits it for the client.Wording stays the same across clients.
Carry forwardAn item that the meeting defers moves to the next meeting's agenda.Open items stay visible.
Action taken reportVCFO lists the decisions of the last meeting and their status.The common item fills itself.
TemplatesSave an agenda as a firm template or a client template.Repeat meetings take one click.
Directors from the client recordVCFO takes the names, DINs and emails of the directors from the client's company record.Notice and attendance use correct data.
7 · Glossary

Words on this page

WordMeaning
Post incorporationThe work that a company must do after the Registrar of Companies registers it. Meetings, registers and filings are part of this work.
Board meetingA meeting of the directors of the company.
General meetingA meeting of the shareholders (members). The AGM occurs every year. An EGM occurs when necessary.
AgendaThe numbered list of items that a meeting discusses. The notice of the meeting includes it.
Common itemAn agenda item that occurs in every meeting of a type, for example "Confirm quorum".
Purpose itemAn agenda item for the purpose of this meeting, for example "Allot shares".
ResolutionA formal decision. A board resolution needs a majority of the directors present. A special resolution of members needs 75% of the votes.
QuorumThe minimum number of people present for a valid meeting.
MinutesThe signed written record of a meeting and its decisions.
Circular resolutionA board decision that the directors approve in writing without a meeting.
MGT-14, DIR-12, PAS-3MCA forms. They report some resolutions, director changes and share allotments to the Registrar.
